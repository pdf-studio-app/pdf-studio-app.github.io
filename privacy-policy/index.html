PDF Studio Privacy Policy

App: PDF Studio (Android, package com.mypdfstudio.app)
Effective date: 6 October 2026

The short version

  • Your PDFs and scans are processed on your phone. The developer never receives them.
  • The developer runs no servers, has no analytics or crash reporting, and has no account system.
  • The camera is used only when you scan a document, to create a PDF on your device.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device information such as the advertising ID to serve and measure ads.

1. Your documents and app data

PDF Studio lets you open, edit and export PDF files. You choose which files the app works with, using Android's file picker. Opening, editing, converting and saving all happen on your device.

The app keeps your settings, and any working copies it needs while you edit, in its private storage on your device.

The developer runs no servers and never receives your documents. There is no analytics or crash-reporting software in the app, and no PDF Studio account.

2. Camera (document scanning)

What permission: CAMERA. The app asks for it only when you start a scan.

Why: to photograph paper documents and turn them into PDFs.

How it works: images are captured and converted to PDF on your device and saved where you choose. They are never sent to the developer or any third party, and are never used for advertising.

Turning it off: revoke the camera permission in Android Settings at any time. Everything except scanning keeps working.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and device and usage information.

  • If you are in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland or another region where it applies, you are asked for consent through Google's consent form. You can change your choice later.
  • If you are in a US state with a privacy law that applies, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your data through Google's privacy message.
  • Ads are limited to a “PG” (parental guidance) content rating or lower, and dating, gambling, get-rich-quick and sexual ad categories are blocked.
  • You can reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings.

Learn more in Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Children

PDF Studio is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect their data.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

The developer holds no user data, so there is nothing for the developer to delete. You control your data:

  • Delete your PDF files to delete the data in them.
  • Uninstall the app to delete everything it keeps in its private storage on your device.

6. Security

Your documents stay on your device. Network traffic for ads is handled by Google over HTTPS.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. When it is, the effective date at the top will change and the new version will be posted at this same address.
