Plumleaf PDF

Edit, sign, merge, compress and convert PDFs on your Android phone. Your files stay on your phone.

Get it on Google Play Free, supported by ads. No account needed.

Private by design

  • Your documents are processed on your phone and never uploaded to the developer.
  • No account, no servers, no analytics: the developer never sees your files.
  • No camera, storage, contacts or location permission. You choose files yourself with Android's file and photo pickers.

What you can do

Edit and sign

Add text, images, dates and your signature. Fill in forms or add your own fillable fields.

Organize pages

Merge, split, extract, remove, reorder and rotate pages, or add blank ones.

Convert

Photos, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX and HTML to PDF. PDF to JPG, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, Markdown and PDF/A.

Compress

Make PDFs smaller to share them faster, keeping text sharp.

Protect

Add a password, remove one you know, or redact text, emails and phone numbers for good.

More tools

Watermarks, page numbers, crop, compare two versions, repair damaged files, summarize and workflows.

Questions

Are my files uploaded anywhere?▾
No. Every tool runs on your phone. The developer has no servers and never receives your documents. See the privacy policy.
Why does the app use the internet?▾
Only to show ads from Google AdMob, which keep the app free. Your documents are never sent over the internet by the app.
Do I need an account?▾
No. There is no sign-in and nothing to register.
Can I open PDFs from other apps?▾
Yes. Choose Plumleaf PDF under "Open with" or "Share" in your file manager, email or messaging app, then pick what you want to do with the file.
