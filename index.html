PDF Studio

A PDF app for Android: open, edit and export PDFs, and scan paper documents into PDFs with your camera.

  • Your documents are processed on your phone and never uploaded to the developer.
  • Scan documents to PDF with the camera. Photos are used only to create your PDF.
  • No PDF Studio account and no servers: the developer never sees your files.
